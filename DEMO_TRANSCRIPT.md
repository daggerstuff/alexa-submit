# Live demo transcript

Captured against the deployed endpoint `https://9jj4zdyhu2.us-east-2.awsapprunner.com/mcp`
(Streamable HTTP, bearer auth). The patient responses below are generated by the
Featherless `Qwen/Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` persona, not hard-coded strings.

Every tool is called over the MCP `tools/call` method with the session's
`mcp-session-id` header. This is the exact sequence to screen-record.

## 1. Initialize

```
server: clinical-conversation-coach v0.3.0
"A scenario-based clinical communication simulator for educational practice."
```

## 2. Tools exposed

```
list_simulation_scenarios  — list scenarios and rubric versions
start_simulation           — start a scenario session
send_practitioner_turn     — send the learner's next utterance
evaluate_simulation        — score the session against the active rubric
end_simulation             — end and return the final evaluation
```

## 3. Start the session

```
start_simulation(session_id="demo-1", scenario_id="chest-pain-basic")
→ scenario chest-pain-basic v1.1.0
```

**Patient:** "Hello. I have been having pressure in my chest and I am worried."
*(emotional state: anxious)*

## 4. Practitioner turns

**Turn 1** — "Where is the pain, and are you short of breath?"

> **Patient:** It feels like pressure right in the middle of my chest. I am a
> little short of breath, but I can still speak in full sentences.
> *(disclosed: chest-pressure, dyspnea — state: concerned)*

**Turn 2** — "When did it start, and does anything make it better or worse?"

> **Patient:** It started about 30 minutes ago. It doesn't seem to get better or
> worse with any particular activity.
> *(state: anxious)*

**Turn 3** — "Are you having any other symptoms, like nausea or sweating?"

> **Patient:** No, I don't feel nauseous or sweaty.
> *(state: anxious)*

## 5. Evaluate

```
evaluate_simulation(session_id="demo-1") → rubric v1.1.0, 11/20
```

| Metric | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction and consent | 1/4 | — |
| Symptom characterization | 4/4 | turn 1, turn 2 |
| Associated symptoms and risk | 4/4 | turn 1 |
| Safety escalation | 1/4 | — |
| Shared next-step confirmation | 1/4 | — |

The low "safety escalation" score is intentional: the transcript demonstrates
that the evaluator flags what the learner did *not* say (urgent escalation for a
concerning chest-pain presentation), which is the evidence-linked feedback loop.

## 6. End

```
end_simulation(session_id="demo-1") → returns the final evaluation for the session
```

## Recording notes

- Keep the whole run under three minutes: one scenario, three turns, evaluate, end.
- Show `tools/list` briefly so the five-tool surface is visible.
- Keep the voice interaction audible; the patient lines are the proof of the
  stateful, constrained persona.
- Do not claim the system diagnoses patients or gives medical advice. The claim
  is narrower: a repeatable, scenario-constrained practice environment.
